Our story

A Sialkot workshop, an international order book

What started as a small embroidery workshop in Sialkot has grown into a name customers across six countries return to.

Who we are

Craft first, always

Malikbros Embroidery, nestled in the heart of Sialkot, Pakistan, has been meticulously crafting exquisite embroidery creations since 2022. Each piece tells a unique story of elegance and refinement, as artisans transform ordinary fabrics into works of art.

With a fusion of traditional hand-stitching and modern machine precision, we breathe life into garments, accessories, and home décor. Whether it's a single bullion badge or a bulk uniform order, the same attention goes into every piece that leaves the workshop.

We proudly serve customers across Pakistan and ship internationally, welcoming custom embroidery orders from clients worldwide.
